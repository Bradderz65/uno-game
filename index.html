          How to play

          The basics

          Match the top card by colour, number or symbol. Wilds can go on anything. First to empty their hand wins.

          House rules at this table

          • Play multiples. Select identical cards (e.g. every 7) and play them together. The last card you pick ends up on top.
          • Stack draws. Any +2, +4 or mega draw can be stacked on another. The next player who can't stack draws the whole pile.
          • Must play if you can. You may only draw when nothing in your hand fits. After drawing, play if you can — otherwise pass.
          • Finish on a number. Your last card can't be an action or wild card.
          • Call UNO. When you're down to one card, press UNO! before playing it — or take two penalty cards.
          • Heads up. With two players, Skip and Reverse both give you another turn.

          Controls

          • Tap cards to select, then Play. Double-tap a card to play it straight away.
          • Press and hold a card to drag it into a new spot.
          • Enter play · D draw / pass · U UNO · S sort · Esc clear
